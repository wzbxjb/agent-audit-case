Methodology

This audit uses digital-marketing-pro v3.2's built-in detection tools to reverse-evaluate content from 4 marketing agents across 8 outputs:

  1. Hallucination Detection — Detects fabricated statistics, fake URLs, unsubstantiated claims, unverified entity references, and definitive claims lacking hedging language
  2. Claim Verification — Extracts factual claims from content and cross-references against a brand evidence file. Classifies each as verified, partially verified, unverified, or contradicted
  3. Output Validation — Validates content structure against type-specific schemas (required sections, word count bounds, format rules, placeholder detection)

Composite scoring weights: Hallucination 40% + Claim Verification 35% + Structure 25%. Hallucination detection carries the highest weight because factual accuracy is the primary gating factor for marketing content usefulness.
