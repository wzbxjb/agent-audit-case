## Image/Video Brief for Creative Team

**Primary asset:** A 15-second vertical video (9:16) shot in a modern living room at dusk.

- **0:00–0:03** — Wide shot of the room with harsh overhead light on. The space feels flat and functional.
- **0:03–0:05** — Overhead clicks off. Brief moment of darkness.
- **0:05–0:12** — Aurora Ceiling Panel fades in with a slow color gradient transition (twilight blue to warm amber). The camera pans slowly across the room, showing how the light reshapes the walls, furniture, and overall mood.
- **0:12–0:15** — End card with product name, "Aurora Ceiling Panel," and "Link in Bio" text overlay.

**Alternate still image:** A top-down flat-lay of a cozy living room corner (rug, low coffee table, book, steaming mug) with the ceiling glowing in soft gradient tones visible in the upper portion of the frame. Shot on a wide-angle lens to capture both the room detail and the ceiling effect.

**Color grading:** Warm and cinematic. Avoid oversaturation — keep the mood natural and inviting, not neon.
